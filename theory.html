The Story

From a home kitchen to the nation's best.

2018
The First Roast

Sam LaRobardiere smoked out his family home roasting beans in the kitchen. He moved to a barbecue grill in the backyard and started selling at Redding farmers markets.

2018
Golden Bean Gold Medal

Won gold in the espresso category at the Golden Bean North America competition. Year one. Roasting on a modified grill.

2019
National Recognition

Good Food Awards Top 10 Overall. Food & Wine Magazine Top 100 Cafés in America. Fresh Cup Magazine Top 30 Roasters in North America.

2019
Twitter HQ Brew-Off

Won the blind tasting competition at Twitter's San Francisco headquarters. Became their sole coffee provider.

Now
Four Locations. 6th in the Nation.

California Street flagship and roastery. Hilltop Drive. Redding Airport. Mt. Shasta. Two Q-Grader certified founders. Weekly cuppings. Direct farm relationships.
