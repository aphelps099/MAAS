The Challenge

Managing Waste at Scale

Five neighboring dairy operations in California's Central Valley faced increasing pressure to manage manure emissions while maintaining economic viability. Traditional waste management methods were costly and contributed significantly to local air quality issues.

The dairies needed a solution that would address environmental concerns, create new revenue streams, and demonstrate environmental stewardship to increasingly conscious consumers.

"MAAS Energy Works understood our challenges from day one. They didn't just bring technology—they brought a partnership approach that made the entire process manageable for our family operation."

John Peterson, Third-generation dairy farmer

Our Solution

Integrated Digester Network

MAAS Energy Works designed and implemented a cluster digester system that collects and processes manure from all five operations at a centralized facility, maximizing efficiency while minimizing individual farm disruption.
