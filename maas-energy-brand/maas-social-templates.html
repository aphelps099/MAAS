Usage Guidelines

Colors

Always use brand colors. Primary green (#2D8B4E) for accents, near-black (#0D1210) for backgrounds.

Logo Placement

Include the MAAS logo on all social graphics. Use white version on dark backgrounds.

Typography

Use Inter for body text, JetBrains Mono for statistics and labels, Georgia for headlines.
