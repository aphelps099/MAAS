Founded in Redding, California

Since 2010, MAAS Energy Works has been at the forefront of America's renewable natural gas revolution. We partner with dairy farmers across the nation to transform agricultural waste into clean energy.

Our integrated approach combines deep technical expertise with strong farmer relationships, creating sustainable solutions that benefit both the environment and rural communities.
